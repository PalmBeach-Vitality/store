Weekly Research Update · August 28, 2026
INDUSTRY UPDATE

One month after the FDA’s Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee recommended six peptides for the 503A Bulk Drug Substances List, the research and compounding communities are watching the next phase closely. The July votes remain non-binding. Formal notice-and-comment rulemaking is still required before any clear legal pathway for compounding exists, a process that historically takes eight to twelve months.

In the interim, demand for research-grade material continues to emphasize verified purity, full COA documentation, and consistent manufacturing standards. Industry observers note that the gap between advisory recommendations and final FDA action creates a period of heightened scrutiny around sourcing, testing, and documentation practices across the peptide space.
