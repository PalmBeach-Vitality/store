Weekly Research Update · September 8, 2026
INDUSTRY UPDATE

Six weeks after the July 23–24 Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee votes, FDA still has not opened notice-and-comment rulemaking on the six peptides recommended for the 503A Bulk Drug Substances List. BPC-157, KPV, TB-500, MOTS-c, Epitalon, and Semax remain advisory-only. Emideltide (DSIP) was not recommended. No Federal Register notice has posted a proposed 503A rule for those substances.

The next confirmed peptide docket is another PCAC meeting by the end of February 2027, covering GHK-Cu, Dihexa acetate, Cathelicidin LL-37, PEG-MGF, and Melanotan II. Until a final rule publishes, compounding pharmacies still lack a clear legal pathway for the July six. Research-use material is a separate channel and is not waiting on that list.

PRODUCT SPOTLIGHT
Semax stays research-grade

Semax was one of the six peptides PCAC recommended, while FDA staff still cited thin characterization and safety data. That gap is why independent third-party testing and a full COA matter more than the vote tally.
