PALM BEACH VITALITY - COMPOUND IDENTIFICATION STRINGS
================================================================================================

Every string the image model is told to print on a product, vials and pens, in one place.
Quoted verbatim from the live sheet mirrors. Nothing here was composed for this file: if a
string looks wrong, fix the sheet, not this file, then regenerate:

    python3 marketing/scripts/build_compound_identification_txt.py

Sources
  vials  marketing/sheets/9-lab-item-creations-500.csv          (535 rows)
  vials  marketing/sheets/500_Peptide_Wellness_Reel_Scenes.csv  (601 rows)
  pens   marketing/sheets/14-pen-creations-150.csv              (168 rows)

A vial carries FOUR printed strings, a pen carries TWO. That is deliberate - the pen prompt
forbids a dose or concentration anywhere in frame, so a pen never shows mg or mg/mL.

SELECTOR vs PRINTED NAME. The left-hand heading is the compound_name you type into
choose_compound. It is not always what gets printed: the blends need a unique selector
because the node matches on a two-way substring. Print name is the quoted string.
